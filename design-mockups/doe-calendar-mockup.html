Card anatomy

  • Navy banner segments the card — date left, time right — like a ticket header.
  • Teal-tint strip below the banner shows the focus area (topic) — full-width, subtle, gives the topic its own layer.
  • Title is the primary reading anchor — the event name itself.
  • Type chip sits below the title, filled with category color and icon (Learning / Convening / Public / Students).
  • Description is a 2-line teaser; full details, audience, and contact live in the click-through modal.
  • Footer has location and a squared Register button. Contact person is modal-only to keep the card focused.
  • Teal accents on Register arrows and contact email links carry the DOE secondary accent forward.
