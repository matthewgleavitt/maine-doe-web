ElevatED 2026

About ElevatED

Registration is now open for the summer 2026 ElevatED professional learning experience! Through a partnership between the Maine Department of Education and Educate Maine/the Teach Maine Center, this event brings together the 2026 Maine DOE Annual Summit and the second annual Every Teacher a Leader Conference for three days of collaboration, inspiration, and high-quality learning for Maine's education workforce.

ElevatED will take place at Thomas College, July 29–31, 2026.

  • Day 1 — July 29: The Annual Summit, tailored for school staff, educators, school safety professionals, and building and instructional leaders.
  • Days 2 & 3 — July 30–31: The Every Teacher a Leader Conference, featuring teacher-led sessions designed specifically for classroom teachers.

Attendees may register for one or both opportunities. Spots are limited and will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis.

About the Annual Summit

In 2022, the Maine Department of Education created the Annual Summit to promote its vision for Maine education:

Workforce & Learning
  • A vibrant and respected educator workforce that innovates, leads, and inspires.
  • Learning environments that are safe and supportive for all.
Student Success
  • A pathway to success in school and life for every student.
  • Schools that foster the potential for all to lead purposeful lives.
Community & Civic Life
  • Schools as trusted centers of the community that foster meaningful civic participation and propel Maine's economy.

We thoughtfully and strategically add components each year to bring more support and information to our educators and partners, with the whole student's needs at the forefront. Allowing adequate time and space for collaboration among attendees is a high priority. This valuable exchange creates a community of support that follows attendees throughout the academic year. Many presentations serve as the launch of continued support offered through the Maine DOE.
