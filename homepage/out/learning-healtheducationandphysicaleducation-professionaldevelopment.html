Links to events and organizations are for reference and information only and do not imply endorsement by the Maine Department of Education.

Maine Department of Education Professional Learning Resources
  • Interdisciplinary Instruction: open-sourced resources, including a mix of video and text options, that support educator growth. Contact hours are available for the self-paced, professional learning modules.
  • MOOSE: a free, optional resource for PreK-12 educators aligned to the Maine Learning Results. Modules have been created in topic areas and themes, including health education and physical education content.
  • EnGiNE: an online library designed to engage innovation in education. Curriculum resources, professional learning modules, and collaboration tools are available for educators to share and develop creative solutions to 21st-century learning challenges.
