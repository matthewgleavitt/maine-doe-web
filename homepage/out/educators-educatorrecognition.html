Maine Educators

Maine is home to many educators and school staff members who make a difference in students' lives and in their communities every single day. The Maine Department of Education (DOE) acknowledges, highlights, and amplifies the work of those who go above and beyond to engage students and the community, inspire colleagues, and strive to improve their practice.

Of the countless education-related recognition programs across the country and in Maine, the Maine DOE directly supports several award programs that recognize Maine's education workforce.

Elevating Exemplary Educators Program

Our Elevating Exemplary Educators program is a nationally aligned initiative to amplify the voices of educators. We seek to build a database of emerging school leaders that consists of a cadre of distinguished teachers, principals, and specialists. In an effort to give educators a seat at the table and tap into their expertise and innovative ideas, these educators may be considered for membership on advisory boards, roundtables, panels, and task forces, as well as candidates for recognition programs. Your recommendations will identify educators who will be of great value to your community and state because of their expertise. In particular, we seek emerging educators relatively new to the profession (5 years or more) who demonstrate unusual instructional and leadership ability. Such persons exhibit strong potential for professional leadership and an inspiring presence that impacts students, colleagues, and community.

Maine Teacher of the Year

Part of the National Teacher of the Year Program, the Maine Teacher of the Year Program honors exceptional classroom teachers across all counties. Each year, Maine selects 16 County Teachers of the Year, one of whom is named Maine State Teacher of the Year. The State Teacher of the Year becomes Maine's nominee for National Teacher of the Year. County and State Teachers of the Year serve as ambassadors for the teaching profession and as advocates for high-quality education in Maine. The Maine DOE administers the Maine Teacher of the Year Program in partnership with Educate Maine.

Milken Educator Awards

Established in 1987, the Milken Educator Awards recognize early-to-mid-career educators for their impressive achievements and potential for future impact. Recipients receive a $25,000 unrestricted award and national recognition for excellence.

Maine History Teacher of the Year

In partnership with the Gilder Lehrman Institute of American History, this program recognizes excellence in K-12 history instruction. Nominations can come from students, parents, fellow educators, or community members.

Recognizing Inspiring School Employees (RISE) Award

Maine participates in the U.S. Department of Education's Recognizing Inspiring School Employees (RISE) Award, which honors classified school employees who provide exemplary service to students from pre-K through high school.
