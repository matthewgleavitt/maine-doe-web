Announcements

Notice: NEO Assessment Roster Discrepancies

This school year, the Maine Department of Education is implementing a new data system called Connect. At this time, we are aware of discrepancies between students enrolled in Connect and the NEO assessment rosters.

The Maine Education Data Management System (MEDMS) Team and the Assessment Team are in close contact and are actively working together to understand and resolve this issue. We will provide additional information as it becomes available. If you have any issue please call or email MEDMS.
