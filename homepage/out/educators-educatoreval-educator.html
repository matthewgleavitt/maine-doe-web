Rule Chapter 180, adopted by the Maine DOE in April 2014, requires that the Department “develop at least one complete State Model PEPG system for teachers and at least one complete State Model PEPG system for principals.” The Teacher Performance Evaluation and Professional Growth (T-PEPG) model was informed by the work of the Maine Schools for Excellence and the development of performance evaluation and professional growth systems in other states. The details of the model elements described in this document are a synthesis of research, conversations, listening and critical review by experts, stakeholders and practitioners in the field.

On April 11, 2019, Governor Mills signed a law that made important changes to Performance Evaluation and Professional Growth (PEPG) systems. Chapter 27, An Act to Amend Educator Evaluation Requirements, includes a number of changes.

  1. Under the new law, school administrative units may choose to identify and include student learning and growth measures that they deem valuable in summative effectiveness ratings, but they are no longer required to do so.  This section of the law went into effect September 1, 2021.  
  2. SAUs must ensure or reconfigure their steering committee membership, such that a majority are teachers who are chosen by a representative of the applicable collective bargaining unit.  This went into effect on September 19, 2019.  
  3. The law states that, “revisions to the performance evaluation and professional growth system made by the steering committee must be reached by consensus.”

Teacher Training Modules

In an effort to help teachers fully participate in the State T-PEPG model in their districts, the Department worked with American Institutes of Research (AIR) to produce a series of training modules intended to inform teachers about the four-step process that structures the State T-PEPG model. Each module, consisting of a PowerPoint slide presentation, a Facilitator’s Guide and handouts, presents interactive learning activities designed within a framework of adult learning theory and best practice. Districts who are either modifying the state model or developing a locally designed Teacher PEPG system can tailor the slides and documents to fit their systems.
