Federal Emergency Relief Programs

The Federal Emergency Relief Funding Dashboard increases the visibility of the services being supported, facilitate public access to information about spending, and highlights the use of federal emergency funding at the local level. The Dashboard empowers all stakeholders – educators, families, and communities – to see allocations, reimbursements requested, and remaining funds.

American Rescue Plan School Operating Status Dashboard

As required by the US DOE, Maine asked each SAU to report the instructional model(s) being offered, attendance, and enrollment data for only APRIL 1, 2021. The dashboard shows data for mode of instruction, enrollment by mode of instruction for all students/race/ethnicity/other student groups, and attendance rate by mode of instruction for all students/other student groups.
