Career and Technical Education

The Maine Department of Education is responsible for the State's oversight and support for the delivery of all career and technical education provided in Maine. This includes the management of the federal Carl D. Perkins grant and the accountability measures that must be reached to receive the funding. The following information and tools will help guide career and technical education (CTE) administrators and educators operate CTE schools and programs.
