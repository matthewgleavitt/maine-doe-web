Interdisciplinary Instruction

Kathy Bertini
Interdisciplinary Instruction Team Coordinator

Supports the following areas:

  • Creating integrated professional learning experiences that connect subjects, strengthen literacy, numeracy, civics, and STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics), and bridge real-world relevance with academic rigor.
  • Designing student-centered, interest-driven instruction aligned with the Whole Student Approach to build engagement, agency, and future readiness.
  • Programming inclusive, interdisciplinary, and data-informed strategies that raise achievement and re-engage learners.
  • Liaising between the Maine DOE and the Maine Curriculum Leaders Association (MCLA) and Maine Principals’ Association (MPA), ensuring alignment, communication, and collaboration across statewide initiatives.
