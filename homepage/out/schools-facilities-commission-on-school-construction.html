Facilities

Per an executive order signed by Gov. Janet Mills, the Governor’s Commission on School Construction will review school construction needs throughout the State of Maine, existing programs that address school facilities needs, and potential new approaches for increasing equity and overall impact. This will be the first such effort in more than 25 years.

Since 2019, the Mills Administration, in partnership with the Maine Legislature, has provided an unprecedented $580 million to finance the renovation and construction of Maine schools. Despite these record investments, the need for construction and renovation of public school facilities in the State has far outpaced available capacity. Additionally, in the years ahead, Maine’s aging school infrastructure is expected to require even more investment.  

Maine students and educators deserve safe, healthy, comfortable, quiet, technology-friendly, functional buildings. The Commission will bring together state, municipal, and education leaders to provide a report with recommendations that outline school construction and renovation needs based on existing data and through input from stakeholders; provide analysis summarizing how other states fund and operate programs supporting public school facilities; and identify potential changes to current state law, rules, and policies controlling school construction.

On April 15, 2025, the Commission issued an Interim Summary of the information and issues reviewed by the Commission thus far. Given the scope and depth of the issues still before the Commission, and in consultation with the Governor’s Office, the Commission will continue its work through 2025, delivering a full and final report—including recommendations—later in the year.
