Adult Education

The U.S. Department of Education Office of Career, Adult, and Technical Education makes grants available to support adult basic education and literacy activities. Funding requires various written reports identifying progress or end-of-year performance.

State-Funded Grants and Programs

The Maine State Legislature funds grants for the Maine College Transition program and also provides state subsidies to support adult basic education, high school completion, and high school equivalency tests.

Policies and Procedures

The Maine DOE Adult Education State Team routinely drafts and updates policies and procedures to offer guidance to directors, educators, and learners to help create a high-quality learning experience. These policies and procedures are intended to improve the efficiency and cooperation among state, local and federal entities as the Maine DOE works to build and maintain Maine’s education resources.
