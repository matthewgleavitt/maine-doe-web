School nurses provide emergency care, preventive services (e.g., immunizations, screenings), and coordinate with families and providers to keep students healthy and ready to learn. The Maine School Nurse Evaluation Tool: School Nurse Competencies and Evaluation helps guide growth, goal setting, and evaluation, aligning with the NASN School Nursing Practice Framework™. Like teachers and administrators, nurses should be supported through professional development and evaluation systems. School nurses operate within both health and education systems, facing unique legal, policy, and ethical challenges. The NASN Code of Ethics offers guidance for practice.

Resources available through Maine DOE and national organizations include:

  • Guidance documents and protocols for student health
  • Professional practice resources for school nurses
  • Additional tools to strengthen school health services
