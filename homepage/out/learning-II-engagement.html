Student engagement is front and center in many educators' minds these days. In this module, hear what current research on engagement says and learn practical strategies for improving student engagement.

Engagement Research

There is a lot of great research about student engagement.  In this video, we briefly cover the psychological model of engagement.  Then we review five enemies of engagement and five ways to counteract them.  This is based on the research by Dennis Shirley and Andy Hargreaves.  A link to their book is listed below.

Continuum of Engagement

Although we often talk about students as either engaged or disengaged, the Continuum of Engagement breaks it down even further.  By taking a more nuanced look at engagement, we can discover ways to improve student engagement.  The video below discusses research on engagement.
