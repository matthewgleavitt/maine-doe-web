Tuition Rates Information

Statute (Title 20-A MRS, Chapter 219) requires that the tuition rates for the current school year are computed using the per-pupil cost of the previous year for each school administrative unit (SAU).

How are the Public School Tuition Rates calculated? Maine Public School Tuition Calculation

When will the tuition rates be available for the current school year?

The calculation cannot be completed until all of the final Quarter 4 financial data for the previous school year has been submitted to the State for every School Administrative Unit; once that data is received, the tuition rates for the upcoming/current school year will be calculated and posted after December 1st.

What can I do about the tuition payment until the tuition rates for the current school year are available?

The tuition rate calculated for the previous year can be used as an estimate for the current year until the new calculation is available.
