The Maine Learning Results, which incorporates the Common Core State Standards, is a single set of clear educational standards for mathematics. These standards are designed to ensure that students graduating from high school are prepared to go to college or enter the workforce, and parents, teachers and students have a clear understanding of what is expected of them.

Standards Documents

A strong mathematics education depends upon a clear understanding of its interrelated concepts, skills, and practices to ensure students are on the pathway to success in their academic careers. The knowledge and skills students need to be prepared for mathematics in college, career, and life are woven throughout the K-12 mathematics performance expectations.
