The people of Maine are lifelong learners served by an innovative, equitable, and learner-centered public education system. Maine honors and values the expertise of support staff, educators, school leaders, and district administrators. Maine teachers have the autonomy to design their instructional experiences and all curriculum decisions are made at the school and school administrative unit level.

From our whole student approach to innovative instructional practices, technology integration, and pioneering outdoor learning initiatives, we invite you to learn more about the opportunities in Maine education.
