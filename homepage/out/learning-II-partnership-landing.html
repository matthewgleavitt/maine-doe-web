Interdisciplinary Instruction

In interdisciplinary learning, it is important for students to see their work in the world around them. Whether that is at the level of the classroom, town, state, or beyond, it helps students share their learning and receive feedback from their communities. These opportunities also allow for community involvement, which is a keystone of the Maine school experience.

Authentic Audience

Authentic audiences are a key part of interdisciplinary instruction since they give students a purpose for their work. This short video gives actionable tips for developing authentic audiences.
