The definition of homeless students are defined in Maine Title 20-A, Part 1, Chapter 1, Definitions, 13-A.

Actually enrolling the student depends on who is actually paying for the student.

If a student is identified as homeless during the current school year you ONLY update night time residence. These examples below are only for the following years enrollment.

Example: Student meets the definition of homeless above, lives in District A, but attends school in District B

If District B is paying for the student, then Option 1 or Option 2 may be used. If District A is paying, then use Option 3

Option 1: 

  • District B enrolls Primary 
  • Resident Town = town within District B 
  • Residency Status = Resident of School Unit

Option 2: 

  • District B enrolls Primary 
  • Resident Town = town within District A 
  • Residency Status = Superintendent Agreement

*The use of the Superintendent Agreement term is for Residency Status, only and does not imply that a "Superintendent Agreement" physically exists.

Option 3: 

  • District B enrolls Primary 
  • Resident Town = town within District A 
  • Residency Status = Paid by Resident SAU
