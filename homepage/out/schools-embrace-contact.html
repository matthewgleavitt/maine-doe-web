Regionalized Programs and Services

The Education Service Centers (ESCs) included here became operational in FY 19. For detailed information about a particular ESC, including contact information for the executive director, a complete membership list, and a menu of services, click on its linked name below.

Membership in a particular ESC does not require the member to be physically located within the ESC's geographic area. However, most ESCs have been formed with members within those boundaries. The geographic areas have been designed around Maine's nine school superintendent's regions listed below: 

  1. Aroostook County
  2. Penquis (Penobscot, Piscataquis, Somerset)
  3. Washington County
  4. Hancock County
  5. Mid-Coast (Knox, Lincoln, Sagadahoc)
  6. Western Maine (Androscoggin, Franklin, Oxford)
  7. Cumberland County
  8. Kennebec Valley (Kennebec, Waldo)
  9. York County

To view current Education Service Centers by location, click on the Education Service Center Interactive Map.
