Wage Rate Requirements for School Construction

State Funded Projects
Projects over $50,000 that include State funding must comply with MDOL Prevailing Wage Rates

  • https://www.maine.gov/labor/labor_stats/publications/wagerateconst/index.shtml

Federally Funded Projects
Projects over $2,000 that include federal funding must comply with Davis-Bacon wage determinations published by USDOL

  • https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/government-contracts/construction
  • https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/IRA
  • Maine DOT has developed “A Contractor’s Guide to Davis-Bacon”PDF
