Child Development Services (CDS)

The nine regional CDS sites serve the various geographical regions of Maine.

Early Intervention (Under Age 3)

For children under three years of age, find the regional site serving the town in which the child lives, or call the State IEU at 877-770-8883.

Part B (Ages 3 to 5)

For children 3 to 5 years old, find the regional site serving the town in which the child lives.
