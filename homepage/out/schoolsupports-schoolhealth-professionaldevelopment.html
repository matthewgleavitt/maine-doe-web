School Health Professional Learning

PureTone Audiometry Screening Training

Maine School Nurses can sign up for this training through the form above. Maine DOE has partnered with the National Center for Hearing Assessment and Management at the University of Utah to provide access to training to prepare school nurses to successfully implement evidence-based Pure Tone Audiometry screenings and follow-up practices through a Learn-to-Screen Training. School nurses in Maine can contact their School Nurse Regional Liaison for more information on how to access the training at no cost.

Vision Screening Guidelines for Maine Schools

To access the learning module, Maine School Nurses must first create an account in the EnGiNE Learning Platform using a Maine school email address. Search for school health learning modules and self-enroll. This module was developed with School Health Associates, LLC to provide nurses in Maine with the knowledge to implement evidence-based vision screenings for students.

Type 1 Diabetes Care in Schools — Maine School Nurse Curriculum

This State of Maine-centered content is part of a larger National Association of School Nurses project funded by the Leona M. and Harry B. Helmsley Charitable Trust. An objective of this project is to create a comprehensive, evidence-based school nurse T1D curriculum and a model for integrating state-specific considerations. The NASN curriculum is undergoing evaluation and will be released in the future.

You can access this in the EnGiNE Learning Platform in the course titled Maine Regulations for Diabetes Management.

Online Learning Resources

School Nursing
  • National Association of School Nurses Learning Center A vast library of training and resources created by and for school nurses.
  • Northeastern University School Health Academy School nursing professional development opportunities.
  • Exploring School Nursing: Scope and Standards of Practice An overview of the 4th Edition of School Nursing: Scope and Standards of Practice, covering how to use standards to guide and evaluate practice.
Mental Health & Student Wellbeing
  • Mental Health Training Interventions for Healthcare Professionals in Schools University of Maryland training system for school health providers managing students with or at risk for emotional and behavioral difficulties.
  • Classroom WISE FREE 3-part training package assisting K–12 educators in supporting student mental health. Includes an online course, video library, and resource collection.
  • Preventing Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs) Trainings to help you understand, recognize, and prevent ACEs across all communities.
Emergency Management & Public Health
  • FEMA Independent Study Free self-paced courses for emergency management. Suggested starting course: IS-100.C Introduction to the Incident Command System.
  • CDC TRAIN A comprehensive catalog of public health training.
  • Maine DHHS Mandated Reporter Training Training for mandated reporters as a critical part of Maine's child protection system.
