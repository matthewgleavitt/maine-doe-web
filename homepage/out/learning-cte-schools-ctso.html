Schools & Programs

SkillsUSA Maine

A partnership of students, teachers, and industry working together to ensure Maine has a skilled workforce. SkillsUSA Maine is the state affiliate of the national organization.

Visit SkillsUSA Maine

National Technical Honor Society (NTHS)

Founded in 1984, NTHS honors the achievements of top CTE students, provides scholarships to encourage higher education, and cultivates excellence in today's skilled workforce.

Visit NTHS

Maine FFA Association

Formerly Future Farmers of America, Maine FFA envisions a future where all agricultural education students discover their passion and chart their course for education, career, and personal growth. Maine FFA has over 400 members statewide and is the state affiliate of the national organization.

Visit Maine FFA

DECA

Formerly known as Distributive Education Clubs of America, DECA prepares emerging leaders and entrepreneurs in marketing, finance, hospitality, and management in high schools and colleges around the globe.

Visit DECA

Maine Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA)

Brings business and education together through innovative leadership and career development programs. Maine FBLA is the state affiliate of the national organization.

Visit Maine FBLA
