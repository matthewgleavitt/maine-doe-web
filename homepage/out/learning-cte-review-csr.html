The standards and procedures that govern career and technical instruction at the secondary level in the State of Maine are set forth in Maine Department of Education Rule Chapter 232, "Standard Criteria for Maine Secondary Vocational Programs."

The comprehensive school review process is designed to serve two interrelated but distinct functions: public accountability and institutional improvement. In 2001, Maine DOE's CTE team researched current standards in technical education review materials and assimilated appropriate items along with concepts and requirements in Rule Chapters 125 and 127, which deal with curriculum, instruction, and assessment.
