Child Development Services (CDS)

The Interagency Coordinating Council (ICC) advises and works collaboratively with Child Development Services (CDS) to provide information, share resources, and identify improvement strategies to help ensure the continued success and improvement of Maine’s comprehensive system of early intervention for infants/toddlers with disabilities and their families.

*If you have any questions about the ICC, or would like to suggest a topic for the ICC to discuss at a future meeting, please contact the ICC Chair, Marnie Morneault, at marnie.morneault@maine.edu.
