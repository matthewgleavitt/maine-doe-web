The MOOSE STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, & Math) Learning Progression is designed to deepen student understanding of science, technology, engineering, art and math topics while exploring how systems in our world are connected. STEAM modules allow students to develop and build upon the following skills:

  • Obtaining, evaluating, and communicating information.
  • Analyzing and interpreting data.
  • Planning and carrying out investigations.

STEAM modules also give students a chance to deepen their understanding of:

  • Patterns
  • Cause and effect
  • The influence of engineering, technology and science on society and the natural world.

Modules in the STEAM Progression are created around the theme “Designing for a Better World”.

Key Themes
  • Designing for a Better World
  • Systems & connections
  • Patterns and cause-and-effect
  • Investigation and evidence
  • Data analysis and communication
STEAM Learning Progression Overview

Explore the Modules

Grades PreK-2

PK-2 modules explore how living things anchor our world.

  • PreK: Forest Fun
  • PreK-K: How can I think like a scientist?
  • PreK-K: Fairytales and the Design Process
  • PreK-K: Gravity Play
  • K: How does my garden grow?
  • G1: Birds, Birds, Birds
  • G1-2: Wind and Water Change our Land
  • G1-2: Design a Playground for Children of All Abilities
  • G2: Animal Actions
Grades 3-5

Grades 3-5 modules look at energy and how living things need and use energy to survive.

  • G3: How does food affect Me?
  • G4: Who was Newton and what's up with his laws?
  • G5: Why do animals need sunlight?
  • G3-5: How can your ideas help plan a new library?
  • G3-5: How does culture help shape celebrations?
Grades 6-8

Grades 6-8 modules dig into resources and the connection between energy, living things, and utilization of resources.

  • G6: Change Begins at Home
  • G7: Do we need trees or do trees need us?
  • G8: How might we design a pizza garden for Maine?
  • G6-8: Underlying Structures in Our World
Grades 9-12

Grades 9-12 modules tie systems together and remind students that systems are needed to support the energy and resources that living things need to survive.

  • G9-10: How does photography impact our lives?
  • G9-12: How much is too much time on social media?
  • G9-12: Got a problem? Use design thinking to solve it.
  • G9-12: Which vehicle power sources are the most beneficial to our future?
  • G11-12: Recycling: Truths, Myths, or Lies?

The modules in this Learning Progression were made possible by a partnership with the Maine Mathematics and Science Alliance.
