The School Construction Committee of the Maine State Board of Education is charged with conducting the Board's business related to its school construction responsibilities. The State Board of Education is required by Maine law to adopt standards governing school construction and to approve all Major Capital School Construction projects for receipt of state funds.

Meeting Information

Unless otherwise noted, meetings are held at 9:00 AM in Room 500 of the Cross State Office Building in Augusta. All meetings are open to the public.
