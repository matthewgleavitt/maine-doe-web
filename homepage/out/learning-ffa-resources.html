Maine FFA Association

The following forms, publications, and resources are related to opportunities with the Maine FFA Association. To see recent Maine recipients of some of these honors, visit the Award Winners web page.

Awards
  • National FFA Chapter Award Recognizes outstanding chapters at the state and national levels.
  • Proficiency Awards Recognize out-of-class-related accomplishments of Maine FFA members.
  • Star Awards Recognition of superior accomplishment by FFA members in out-of-class activities. Applicants must also have completed a State FFA Degree application.
  • Chapter FFA Advisor of the Year — Nominate Today Recognize the outstanding performance of a chapter advisor.
  • Outstanding Chapter Officer Recognition based on nomination by chapter FFA advisor of a chapter officer with exceptional qualities and accomplishments.
