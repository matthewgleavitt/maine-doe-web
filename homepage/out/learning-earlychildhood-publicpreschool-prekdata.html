Currently, public Pre-K in Maine is available for 4-year-olds in approximately 91% of school administrative units or SAUs. While the cognitive and social-emotional benefits of high-quality Pre-K are well documented, there are a variety of factors that contribute to whether school systems offer public Pre-K and the extent to which they are able to enroll all eligible and interested 4-year-olds.

Below are visual representations of current public Pre-K data in Maine. Each data set offers a brief description.

Additionally, every year the Department of Education asks school administrative units (SAUs) to complete an annual Pre-K survey. This survey helps the Maine DOE to update data related to Public Pre-K.

Over the last ten years there has been a steady increase in public Pre-K enrollment. Currently public schools in Maine serve approximately 64% of 4-year-olds in public Pre-K. This is calculated by dividing the Pre-K enrollment by the kindergarten enrollment.

**PreK 25 26 number of classrooms data photo**

As of the most current school year (2025-2026), approximately 91% of SAUs offer a public Pre-K option for their communities. Of those 178 SAUs, 70% report universal enrollment in Pre-K.

The percentage of SAUs that offer full day/full week programming continues to increase as more SAUs build their capacity.
