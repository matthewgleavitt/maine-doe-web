Maine Adult Education is a public education and career pathways system that enables adults to be prepared for postsecondary education and/or employment. Over 60 adult education programs throughout Maine provide a range of instructional services to help adults develop the skills for further educational opportunities, job training, and better employment, and to realize their full potential as productive workers, family members, and citizens.

Maine Adult Education

Adults seeking to further their education or advance their career can enroll in adult education courses in literacy and adult basic education, high school completion, English language acquisition, college preparation, career preparation, and enrichment. Instruction is aligned with adult learners' goals and focused on supporting their efforts to meet the College and Career Readiness Standards for Adult Education (CCRS), developed for the Office of Career, Technical, and Adult Education (OCTAE) and adopted by the Maine adult education system.

The Maine adult education system partners with the Maine Adult Education Association (MAEA), Maine CareerCenters, local employers, higher education, and community agencies to maximize the education and training opportunities for its adult learners.

Adult Education: A Stepping Stone to New Careers

From July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2025, over 12,400 adult learners participated in Maine adult education programming in one or more of the following areas: High School Completion, College Preparation, and/or Workforce Training and Development. During the 2024-25 academic year, 1,113 adult education participants earned a high school credential and adult learners received over 3,000 certifications and credentials of value in high-demand career fields.
