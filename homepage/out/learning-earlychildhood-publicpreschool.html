Welcome to Public Preschool in Maine. Our focus is on expanding access to high-quality public pre-k programming for all 4-year-old students through a whole student and community approach. In Maine, it is a local decision for school administrative units (SAUs) to develop and implement a public preschool program (also called a public pre-k). The Early Learning Team at the Maine Department of Education is happy to offer support and information to SAUs that currently offer public pre-k programs and to those looking to implement programs.
