Support from the Dana Center

The Launch Years Initiative, led by the Charles A. Dana Center at the University of Texas at Austin, supports the scaling of mathematics pathways from high school through postsecondary education and into the workplace, aligned to students’ goals and aspirations. Maine is among twenty-two states that have joined this work, along with national organizations and leaders in mathematics education. At the heart of this work is that for far too many students, math is a wall—not a way—to their postsecondary and career success. In fact, the Mathematical Association of America calls math “the most significant barrier” to finishing a degree—and ultimately to a path of greater opportunity for all students. In Maine we are working together across public education to change the experiences and outcomes for students.
