Project-based learning (PBL) and other models, such as play-, problem-, or place-based, are powerful instructional models that engage students in the community around them. Embedding literacy instruction into PBL is aligned with reading science and can be a powerful combination. The following module seeks to connect educators with PBL practices that support literacy instruction. In this module, you will find:

  • a definition of project-based learning
  • a connection between foundational literacy and PBL
  • PBL literacy moves based on the Active View of Reading

Literacy Foundations & the Active View of Reading

Language Comprehension

One of the most powerful instructional moves to support literacy is activating prior knowledge and building background knowledge.  Prior knowledge is everything a student knows about a topic and may be tied to their culture.  For example, students may have prior knowledge about holidays that they celebrate.  That knowledge will help them comprehend holiday books.
