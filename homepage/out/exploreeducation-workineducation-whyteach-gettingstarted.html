An educator preparation program is where you gain the skills to teach. In an educator prep program, the teaching program is embedded in the bachelor's degree you earn. Knowing the credentialing requirements to becoming certified to work in Maine can be helpful as you make decisions regarding which educator prep program is a good fit for you. Credentials

Maine DOE has a strong Certification Team dedicated to supporting educators throughout their careers. Background checks are required for any role in Maine education. Specific requirements for certification vary from credential to credential. Maine offers pathways to certification thus honoring the unique journey of the individual.
