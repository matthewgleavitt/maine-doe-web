Career and Technical Education

Career and technical education (CTE) is governed by State and federal regulations. The federal compliance office is the Office of Vocational and Adult Education. The State Board of Education is the state governing board.

Regulations
  • State: 
    - Chapter 313 of Title 20-A  
    - Program Approval, Chapter 231
    - Standard Criteria, Chapter 232
    - Program Evaluation, Chapter 236
    - Live/Outside Work, Chapter 237 
    - Regional School Calendars 
    - Equal Educational Opportunity, Chapter 4
  • Federal: All CTE schools are subject to the federal Carl D. Perkins Act and the Education Department General Administrative Regulations.  
Methods of Administration (MOA)

The Department conducts CTE site visits as part of its Vocational Education MOA civil rights compliance of districts that receive federal funding.
