Home Instruction

Parents new to home instruction should review the Home Instruction Requirements, Forms, and Laws webpage. If your question is not covered there or on this page, please check with your local superintendent's office.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most recent home instruction guidance updates?

A Reminder to Maine Home Instruction Parents and SAUs Regarding Annual Assessment Requirement

What is the web address for the Home Instruction Portal?

https://neo.maine.gov/DOE/NEO/Dashboard

When should a parent or legal guardian submit a Notice of Intent to Provide Home Instruction?

A Notice of Intent must be filed annually by the parent or legal guardian. A school year starts July 1 and ends June 30. For parents in the first school year of home instruction in Maine, file the notice within 10 days of withdrawal from school or by September 1 if beginning with the new school year. Parents of students continuing home instruction after the previous school year must submit a prior year assessment together with the Notice of Intent by September 1.

Must I use the Home Instruction Portal or the official Notice of Intent form?

Using the Home Instruction Portal is not mandatory. You may provide notice to the superintendent's office in a different format, provided it contains all required information:

  • Applicable school year
  • Parent/guardian full name
  • Physical address, city, zip code; and mailing address (if different)
  • Child's full name
  • Child's date of birth or age expressed in years
  • Indicate first or subsequent year of home instruction
  • Date starting home instruction (if first year)
  • First year assurance: The home instruction program will provide at least 175 days annually of instruction in the following subject areas: English and language arts, math, science, social studies, physical education, health education, library skills, fine arts and, in at least one grade from grade 6 to 12, Maine studies. At one grade level from grade 7 to 12, the student will demonstrate proficiency in the use of computers. The program will include an annual assessment of the student's academic progress as described in 20-A M.R.S. §5001-A(3)(A)(4)(b).
  • Prior year assessment (if subsequent year)
  • Subsequent year statement: I intend to continue providing home instruction and enclose the prior year annual assessment of the student's academic progress as outlined in 20-A M.R.S. 5001-A(3)(A)(4)(b).
  • Signature and date of signature
  • A valid email address if you would like an acknowledgment
How do I find my local school superintendent's address?

You will find an up-to-date list of addresses on our website.

Do I need to file a Notice of Intent for my 5-year-old child?

According to Maine law, school attendance is compulsory from the 6th birthday to the 17th birthday.

Does the parent receive acknowledgment after submitting a Notice of Intent?

Yes, an acknowledgment is sent via email, provided the parent has supplied a valid email address.

Do annual assessment results have to be submitted together with the Notice of Intent?

Yes — for students who have completed the previous school year in home instruction in Maine, assessment results are due by September 1, submitted together with the Notice of Intent. Assessments must be attached to the paper notice or uploaded during submission to the Home Instruction Portal. Notices submitted without annual assessment results will be flagged for follow-up.

Is the parent or legal guardian required to submit an annual assessment every year?

Yes, every year after the first year of home instruction. Even if the student completes the school year as a home instruction student but will not be participating in home instruction in the subsequent year, annual assessment results are still required. Parents are requested to include a note along with the assessment results stating that the student will no longer be participating in home instruction, and the date that they exited home instruction.

If my child is enrolled in a distance learning program, do I need to file annual assessment results?

Yes, unless the student is enrolled in one of Maine's two virtual public charter schools — Maine Connections Academy or Maine Virtual Academy. A Notice of Intent with annual assessment results is required as usual if your child is enrolled in a distance learning program outside of one provided by the resident local school.

What should a parent do if the student ceases participating in home instruction during the school year?

If home instruction ceases and the child is still of compulsory school attendance age, parents must comply with attendance laws in some other way. As a courtesy, please notify the superintendent and school of any changes.

How many days and hours of instruction are required?

A total of at least 175 instructional days are required. There is no hourly requirement, per day or per year.

If a parent begins home instruction during the school year, must they provide at least 175 instructional days?

The number of days that the student attended school prior to beginning home instruction can be deducted from the 175 instructional day requirement.

How does a parent retrieve or edit their submitted home instruction forms?

A parent cannot retrieve or edit their home instruction information except through a request to the school administrative unit's superintendent's office. Because of privacy, all requests must be made by the parent who submitted the Notice (or by the former student) and are subject to signed release and verification.

Can a home instruction parent provide age in years instead of date of birth?

Yes.

Can a home instruction parent provide a PO Box instead of a physical address?

No. The physical address determines the school administrative unit of residence and is required.

Who gets notified when a parent submits home instruction information via the Portal?

Both the parent (if a valid email address is provided) and the superintendent's office receive acknowledgments.

Is the Home Instruction Portal information relayed securely?

Yes.

What happens if a submitted assessment does not comply with one of the approved methods outlined in the statute?

If a submission is noncompliant, the superintendent's office will follow up with the parent for the missing information.

Is there a fee to provide home instruction in Maine?

There is no fee charged. However, parents bear the responsibility for all costs.

What instructional materials are provided to home instruction students by the local school?

The local school may provide textbooks upon request as long as extra copies are available. The local school is not required to provide additional instructional materials such as workbooks, lesson plans, computers, etc.

Are home instruction students eligible to receive laptops through the Maine Learning Technology Initiative?

No, this program is not available to home instruction students.

Can a parent file a Notice of Intent to Provide Home Instruction for just one course?

No.

What are the curriculum standards for each grade level?

Subjects must include English, language arts, math, science, social studies, physical education, health education, library skills, fine arts, Maine Studies (in at least one grade from 6 to 12), and proficiency in the use of computers (at one grade level from 7 to 12). There are no specific curriculum standards for Maine home instruction.

Will the state of Maine issue a diploma or certificate of completion?

No. Since there are no standards of achievement for home instruction in Maine, the state does not issue grades, credits, diplomas, transcripts, letters of course completion, or letters of grade level promotion. Parents are advised to contact post-secondary institutions, military recruiters, or potential employers to inquire about what will be required for acceptance, admission, and employment.

When should the parent or the school administrative unit contact the Department?

Parents and superintendent offices may contact the Maine DOE with questions or issues with the home instruction portal, or with general home instruction questions.
