Step-by-Step for Liaisons
  • Determine eligibility under McKinney-Vento — if needed, use MV Screener Form Option 1. Document status in the student information system.
  • If the student is unaccompanied, determine caregiver if applicable. Use the Caregiver Template for general educational decisions.
  • If the child is MV, unaccompanied, and identified for Special Education, set up an Educational Surrogate Parent.
  • Determine best interest for school placement in partnership between the SAUs of origin and residence. Best Interest Determination Interview Checklist — Parents
  • Immediately enroll in school of residence without normally required documents, or arrange transportation for the child to remain in school of origin in partnership between SAUs.
  • Provide family/unaccompanied homeless youth/caregiver with information on their right to Dispute Resolution.
  • Determine child's needs to succeed in school — Sample MV Check-In Form / Student Needs AssessmentWord.
  • Connect to referrals based on identified needs. For unaccompanied homeless youth: and FAFSA Unaccompanied Homeless Youth Sample Form.
