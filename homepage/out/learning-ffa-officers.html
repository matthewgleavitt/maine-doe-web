The Maine FFA Association is a student-run organization directed by a team of student state FFA officers with support from the Maine FFA Association and FFA Liaison.

The state FFA officers of the Maine FFA Association have demonstrated a superior level of commitment to Maine FFA, as well as to agriculture and natural resources. All officers have participated in high-quality leadership training to prepare them for their year of service. During that year, the officers conduct FFA presentations in classrooms, develop and deliver student leadership workshops, represent the Maine FFA and agricultural education at state- and national-level events, and prepare and present the annual State FFA Convention at the University of Maine. During the convention, student FFA members are able to participate in competitive events, tours, and award sessions, receiving recognition for their accomplishments.
