Maine School Safety Center Services

Our Maine Safe Schools Initiative prioritizes Supporting a Restorative Culture of Care and Safety in schools by providing training, guidance, and ongoing support for the whole school and the whole student. We approach school safety through the lens of prevention and response with the overarching goal of helping educational leaders create a safe environment for students and staff where everyone feels like they matter. This environment is created through intentional action that identifies and addresses potential problems before they arise. Prevention starts by building community, creating and practicing safety plans, and understanding how to recognize warning signs of possibly harmful behavior. These skills are addressed in the following MSSC programs:

School Emergency Management

School Safety Specialist Badge Series

  • Introduction
  • Fundamentals
  • School Climate & Culture
  • Emergency Planning
  • Special Considerations
  • Incident Command System

This program is offered asynchronously through Eastern Maine Community College. It takes approximately 20 hours to complete over an 8-week period. Participants are given badges after each stage.

Behavioral Threat Assessment and Management

Comprehensive School Threat Assessment Guidelines (CSTAG)

Team-based

  • Instructor-led Level 1
  • Instructor-led Level 2

Schools select a team of participants to enroll in CSTAG training. Level 1 and Level 2 are facilitated by an instructor. This program takes approximately 8 hours to finish. 

Restorative Practices

Creating a Culture of Care in Schools Using Restorative Practices

Whole School

  • Asynchronous Modules
  • In-person Facilitated by Team

Schools sign up to participate in this program as a whole staff. Schools select a team of leaders to undergo training from a coach. The staff are trained asynchronously at a pace chosen by the school.

When challenges do arise, guidelines for safe, student- centered responses are also imbedded in both the CSTAG and Creating a Culture of Care in Schools Using Restorative Practices training. The information provided helps school staff to repair harm after an incident, while safely reintegrating students back into the community after any prolonged absence.
