Resources - Career and Technical Education

Individuals wishing to become CTE teachers have a number of paths they can follow to reach that goal. Below are resources to assist in that process.

NOTE: All decisions regarding Teacher Certification/Endorsement within the State of Maine are handled through the Maine Certification Team.

  • CTE Endorsement RequirementsExcel
  • Chapter 115 Provides a brief outline of the five different pathways that one may follow to become a CTE instructor. Choose "Part II" and jump to page 44.
  • Certification Team: This link goes directly to the DOE Certification Office web page.
  • Courses available at EMCC: This is one of the places where CTE teacher certification courses are provided.
  • NOCTIPDF: To request a NOCTI instructor assessment for certification purposes, complete the NOCTI Assessment Request Form and submit to Danielle Despins-Kelley at Danielle.r.Despins@maine.gov.
