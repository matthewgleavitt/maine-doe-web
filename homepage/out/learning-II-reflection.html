Interdisciplinary Instruction

Reflective practice is all about taking a step back to think about what went well, what didn't, and what we can do differently next time. It's a simple but powerful way to grow. This module describes educator-focused reflective practices from two perspectives — reflecting on a lesson or unit, and using data to develop a reflective focus for future planning.
