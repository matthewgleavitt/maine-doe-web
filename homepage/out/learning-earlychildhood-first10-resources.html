First 10: Schools and Communities in Maine

There are a variety of approaches for designing and developing First 10 Communities. The links below provide access to well-researched designs, all of which incorporate several key principles — public schools serve a central role; attention is paid to aligning learning across the birth to grade 5 spans; and children and families are connected to wrap-around services that enhance the quality of learning and care.
