Creating a Culture of Care in Schools Using Restorative Practices

Welcome to the Creating a Culture of Care in Schools Using Restorative Practices Resource Center — a collection of supplementary materials for educators who have completed the training. Included are additional resources to support schools across Maine as they expand environments of safety and mattering for staff and students, as well as connections to community partners that can help with some of the trickiest challenges.

All materials are currently available as downloadable PDFs. The goal is to continue developing and expanding the resources offered here and to grow a new Community of Practice in Maine. Feedback is important for shaping what is included — please take a moment to share thoughts in the anonymous survey below.

Thank you for all you do for the students of Maine!

Dr. Stacey Gulley-Vernon
Restorative Practices Coordinator, Maine School Safety Center
