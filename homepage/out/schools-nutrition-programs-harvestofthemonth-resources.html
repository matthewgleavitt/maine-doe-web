Maine Harvest of the Month

The Maine Department of Education, Child Nutrition Harvest of the Month materials are intended solely for use by the Maine Department of Education, Child Nutrition Office and sponsors participating in the National School Lunch Program, Summer Food Service Program, and Child and Adult Care Food Program. Any other individual, organization, or entity wishing to use these materials must obtain prior written permission from the Maine Department of Education, Child Nutrition Office.
