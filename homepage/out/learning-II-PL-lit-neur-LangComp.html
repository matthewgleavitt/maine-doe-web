Integration of Literacy and Neuroscience Home Page

Much of the Language Comprehension section is similar to Scarborough's Rope. Newer categories, found in the Active View of Reading based on reading science, are Cultural and Other Content Knowledge and Theory of Mind. Cultural and Other Content Knowledge includes work on the role of students' prior and background knowledge to improve reading. Theory of Mind refers to a reader's ability to empathize with characters or people in books.

Each accordion below has self-paced asynchronous professional learning modules with a mix of video and text resources, opportunities for application and reflection, and contact hours.
