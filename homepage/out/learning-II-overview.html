The II team's vision is to create a future where changemakers are equipped with the dispositions, conceptual understandings, skills, and knowledge necessary to tackle global challenges through interdisciplinary instruction.  The II team believes that embracing interdisciplinary pedagogies can break down the barriers between traditional academic disciplines and foster a more whole-child approach to learning.  This approach empowers the next generation of thinking and leaders to think critically, collaborate across disciplines, and innovate solutions that address the interconnected social, economic, and environmental issues facing our world today.
