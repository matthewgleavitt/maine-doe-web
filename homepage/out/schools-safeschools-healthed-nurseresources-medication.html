Medication administration is an essential service schools provide to support student health and learning. The complexity of medication administration in the school environment continues to evolve due to various factors, and students may require medications for various reasons. The school nurse is often the only health professional in the building. The school nurse selects and trains UAPs annually and continually evaluates them for competence in performing a selected nursing task, such as medication administration. The medication training videos provided are intended to augment the school nurse's training, not replace it.

The Maine Department of Education provides the following guidelines in accordance with Maine Revised Statute Title 20-A section 6403-A (5), which directs the commissioner to issue guidelines on the provision of school health services and health-related activities.
