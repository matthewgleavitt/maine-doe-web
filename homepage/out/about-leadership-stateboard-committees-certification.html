The Certification & Higher Education Committee of the Maine State Board of Education is charged with handling aspects of Board business related to the degree-granting authority of institutions of higher education, state certification requirements for educators, and approval of educator preparation programs.

Report as per L.D. 485, Resolve, 2023, Chapter 54

Meeting Information

Unless otherwise noted, meetings are held from 10:30 AM to 12:00 PM in Room 538 of the Cross State Office Building in Augusta. The committee generally meets on the fourth Wednesday of each month.
