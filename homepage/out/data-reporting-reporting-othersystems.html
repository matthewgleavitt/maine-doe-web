Diploma Sender

Request GED Transcripts/Diplomas

Students and programs in Maine can request GED and HiSET transcripts or diplomas using the DiplomaSender website. DiplomaSender provides a single-source, internet-based service to accurately manage all high school equivalency testing and diploma data in an environment that is secure and accessible.
