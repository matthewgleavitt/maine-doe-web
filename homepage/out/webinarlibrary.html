Welcome to the Maine Department of Education professional learning webinar library. Here you can find a series of recorded webinars and training across a broad scope of topic areas shared by Maine DOE staff and endorsed partners.

If you have questions about contact hours for professional development, please reach out to the individual responsible for that content area. 

Certain opportunities and recorded trainings may qualify for contact hours, but not all video content from Maine DOE is eligible. Please reach out to a staff member prior to engaging with the content to know if it will qualify.
