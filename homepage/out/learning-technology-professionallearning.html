Maple Bytes Professional Learning Modules

Maple Bytes is a Maine Department of Education–developed interdisciplinary instructional program that invites students to explore the science and cultural significance of maple syrup through a unique blend of Wabanaki Studies, Computer Science, and Earth Science. Designed by Maine DOE’s specialists Allison Braley, Erik Wade, and Brianne Lolar, this lesson provides a rich, place-based learning experience grounded in both traditional knowledge and modern technology.

AI Guidance Toolkit for Schools and Educators

This dynamic resource goes beyond traditional policies, pairing guidance with learning by offering practical tools, real-world scenarios, and ongoing learning opportunities that support ethical, responsible, and effective AI use. Grounded in digital citizenship and computer science foundations, this toolkit is designed to enhance the understanding and application of artificial intelligence in education.
