Math Pathways in Maine

Math pathways work at the secondary level supports every student to learn rigorous, engaging, and relevant mathematics that will facilitate their future academic and career pursuits. Several Maine school administrative units (SAU) have already begun making changes to support these goals. The examples below highlight different features of and approaches to math pathways work in Maine.

  • MSAD 11: Strategic Planning on the Road to Math Pathways This profile features MSAD 11 and explains their strategic vision and efforts to bring about real change in mathematics teaching and learning in their SAU.
  • Brewer School Department: Flexible Options for High School Mathematics Courses This profile features Brewer High School, which has been offering increased flexibility in math course options.
  • MSAD 6: Mathematics in Career & Technical Education This profile features Bonny Eagle High School and explains how they have worked to support students’ math learning and related credits within their CTE programs.
  • RSU 16: Data Science This profile features Poland Regional High School's innovative approaches to expand electives that are available to students including data science.
