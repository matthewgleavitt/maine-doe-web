The Legislative Team supports the overall vision, mission, and strategic priorities of the Maine Department of Education in a variety of ways, including those below.

Legislative Team

Liaising with the State Legislature
  • Provides information regarding Department programs and Maine's education system to the Education and Cultural Affairs Committee
  • Shepherds Department opinions regarding proposed legislation
  • Supports the development of proposed legislation at the Department
Collaborating with DOE Offices
  • Supports the preparation of Department testimony
  • Provides logistical support for legislatively established work groups, task forces, and reports
  • Provides information regarding legislation, rules, and statutory language to colleagues, including legislative session summaries of education-related bills
Representing Maine's Interests in Federal Contexts
  • Builds collegial working relationships with Maine's federal legislators
  • Works collaboratively with departments in other states to inform best practices
Leading Rulemaking Efforts
  • Gathers individuals with a vested interest and leads conceptual conversations
  • Solicits public comment
  • Drafts proposed rules or rule changes
Implementing Work Groups, Task Forces, and Legislative Reports
  • Collaborates with Office Leads to complete legislatively required work groups, task forces, and legislative reports
  • Provides logistical and facilitation support for convenings of individuals with a vested interest for work groups and task forces
  • Ensures timely and thorough completion of all reports
Providing Constituent Service
  • Provides information on how to get involved in the legislative process
  • Answers constituent questions regarding statutes
  • For general questions, comments, and concerns regarding education in Maine, use the Contact Us form and your question will be directed to the most knowledgeable person
