Directions

Traveling north or south on Interstate 95, take Exit 109. Yield right onto Western Avenue (Route 17/202) and travel straight until you reach the rotary. Take the second exit off the rotary. Travel across Memorial Bridge that spans the Kennebec River; at the end of the bridge you will come to another rotary bear right onto Stone Street (Route 9/17). Travel up Stone Street (Route 9) stay in right lane to first set of lights, go straight to the 4-way intersection with second traffic light stay straight on Stone Street (Route 9), travel approximately 5/10th of a mile to the third traffic light, turn right into the AMHI Complex (the brick buildings on the right). Take the first driveway on your right. Parking is available in the large parking lot on the right, just beyond the Deering Building.

We are located on the 1st floor and you can enter on the end of the building by the granite wall.
