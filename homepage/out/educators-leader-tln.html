Increasing student achievement through supporting leadership growth

TLN registration is now open for the 2026-2027 school year!

The Maine Department of Education (Maine DOE) invites school principals to register for a year-long professional learning experience. The Transformational Leaders' Network (TLN) is a practical, relationship-centered professional learning opportunity for school leaders to enhance their leadership practice and strengthen outcomes for schools and communities. The TLN occurs through a series of in-person and remote/regional meetings offered across the state during the school year. All principals, from the novice to the veteran and from large suburban schools to small rural schools, are invited to participate.

New this year: a Leadership Series for Assistant School Leaders — see Strengthening Leadership from Within below.

About the Transformational Leaders' Network

The Transformational Leaders' Network (TLN) creates an ongoing community of principals engaged in sustained professional learning that strengthens leadership identity, emotional intelligence, organizational capacity, and long-term sustainability.

The most important aspects of TLN can be summed up in these words: connection and community, learning and reflection, and safety and support. Rather than a typical professional development program, TLN is a professional lifeline, providing a trusted community where school leaders find belonging, clarity, and collective wisdom.

Each session involves sharing challenging issues, experiencing tools for leading at your school, and exploring relevant leadership literature in a community of like-minded learners. TLN emphasizes practical leadership habits and skills that strengthen effectiveness while reducing unnecessary burden.

The 2026-2027 TLN theme is "The Principal Effect and the Power of Mattering." This opportunity will reflect current research regarding principal effectiveness, professional learning, leadership sustainability, emotional intelligence, systems thinking, and school climate, while honoring the realities of increasingly complex school leadership.

TLN Highlights
  • Continuous job-embedded professional development for an increasingly complex role.
  • A collaborative learning community, as participants tackle authentic leadership challenges together.
  • Learning about what emotional intelligence, mattering, and leadership presence looks like in practice.
  • Strategies for strengthening school climate and culture by building trust, establishing stronger connections, communicating openly, and supporting and engaging staff more effectively.
  • Learning about creating steadier school environments during periods of challenge and change through sustainable and systems-oriented leadership.
  • Learning about navigating conflict, pressure, and complexity.
  • Learning about staff ownership, increasing collective efficacy, supporting leadership succession, and strengthening workforce.

Registration for the 2026-2027 TLN

Transformational Leaders' Network Registration 2026-27

The TLN registration fee is $295 per participant for the full year of professional learning, including all TLN content, activities, and materials.

If you are the leader of an identified school under Maine's Model of School Supports (MMSS) — TSI (Targeted School Improvement), ATSI (Additional Targeted School Improvement), or CSI/CSI-R (Comprehensive School Improvement) — please contact Christina O'Neal at Christina.l.Oneal@maine.gov for a discount code to cover your registration fees.

Participants will be responsible for travel. Scholarship funds will be available to assist with travel expenses, with scholarships awarded on a first-come, first-served basis prioritizing need, utilizing a CTB process and specific eligibility and fund guidelines. If interested in learning more about scholarship funding, please email Christina O'Neal at Christina.l.Oneal@maine.gov.

New Leadership Series for Assistant School Leaders

Strengthening Leadership from Within

A Leadership Series for Assistant Principals, Deans of Students, and Lead Members of Leadership Teams

Two In-Person Sessions + One Virtual Connection
Facilitated by Maine's Transformational Leaders' Network (TLN)

"Professional learning opportunities for school leaders can build leadership capacity and develop leaders' competence across the full range of their responsibilities—empowering them to foster environments in which adults and students thrive." — Learning Policy Institute & Wallace Foundation (2026)

Session 1 (In Person) — November 6, 2026, 8:30–2:30, location TBD
Leading Beyond Position: Building Presence, Influence, and Leadership Identity

Assistant principals, Deans of Students, and Leadership Team members will explore:

  • Transitioning from expert practitioner to systems leader
  • Emotional intelligence and leadership presence
  • Building trust and credibility
  • Supporting adults while managing competing priorities
  • Understanding influence beyond authority

Session 2 (90-min. Virtual Connection) — February 5, 2027
Learning from Practice: Conversations with Maine Principals

Participants will engage in facilitated conversations centered on authentic leadership challenges, including:

  • Difficult conversations
  • Managing complexity and middle-management pressures
  • Supporting school culture
  • Building staff leadership
  • Protecting sustainability in leadership work

Session 3 (In Person) — May 14, 2027, 8:30–2:30, location TBD
Creating Conditions Where Others Grow

Participants will explore:

  • Building teacher leadership
  • Strengthening staff and student mattering
  • Shared decision-making
  • Developing climate and culture
  • Leadership succession and sustainability

Through this work, TLN intends to strengthen Maine's leadership pipeline while reinforcing the belief that effective leadership development begins long before the principalship itself.

The cost of the Leadership Series for Assistant School Leaders is $120 per participant. This includes all content, materials, and meals for in-person meetings. Participants will be responsible for covering their own travel.
