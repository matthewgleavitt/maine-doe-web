Bomb Threat Reporting & Laws

Mandatory Online Bomb Threat Reporting The Maine Department of Education's secure school bomb threat reporting database requires administrators to have a username and a case-sensitive password to report incidents. Your local Superintendent Office should have the username and password on file.

Related Laws

  • Response to school bomb threats, Section 263
  • School bomb threat response policies, Section 1001.17
  • Bomb threat information in student handbooks, Section 1001.18

Emergency Operations Planning (EOP)

The MSSC will provide training, guidance, and ongoing support to schools in order to assure every school in Maine possesses a comprehensive, high-quality school emergency operations plan.

Organizations and resources included are for reference and information only; they are not an endorsement from the Maine Department of Education.
