Supervision, Monitoring, and Support - SAUs

Federal Regulations dictate correction of each instance of noncompliance as soon as possible but in no case longer than one year. (OSEP Q&A 23-01)

Monitoring Process

  1. Self Assessment: Districts complete the 2026-2027 Self Assessment form.  Each SAU is sent a link specific to them in June.  Access a model/practice form here.
  2. Site Visit: The team will come onsite to verify information reviewed as part of the Self Assessment as well as forms, policies, and federal indicator data.  The date of the onsite visit is included in the Letter of Notification, June email.
  3. Next Steps:
    1. Pre-findings, if applicable, correction within 30 days (optional).
    2. Child-specific findings related to Abbreviated Day documentation must be corrected within 30 days.
    3. Summary of Findings  And Corrective Action Plan 
      1. Issued on January 31 for site visits November-December.
      2. Issued on June 30 for site visits April-May.
    4. CAP Specific training And support as needed throughout the year.

Please note that the process varies for Communities Without Schools (CWS) and Regional Programs.  Please see Monitoring Timeline and Resources for more information.
