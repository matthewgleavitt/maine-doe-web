Title I, Part C - Highly Mobile Students

Contact

Title I, Part C is an educational program that supports migratory agriculture and fishing workers and their families to minimize the potential impact of family mobility on school success. The Maine Title I, Part C – Highly Mobile Students Program provides the following Eligibility Screener to all Maine school districts to help locate families that may qualify for related services. Families can elect to complete this form in their primary language.

Each school district is responsible for sending out a Title I, Part C Eligibility Screener for every student to complete every year as part of their school registration packet.

If all three (3) questions are answered "Yes", a copy of the screener is sent to the DOE for follow up and the screener is kept on file in the student's records. Schools should shred forms that are answered "No". The local Title I, Part C Regional Coordinator follows up with all families for whom we receive an Eligibility Screener to determine eligibility for Title I, Part C and provide supplemental services based on their individual needs.

Community Survey

The Maine Title I, Part C – Highly Mobile Students Program provides the following Community Survey to Maine Community Organizations to help locate families that may qualify for related services. Any community organization in the state of Maine can include this survey as part of their onboarding, registration or enrollment packets. If all three (3) questions are answered "Yes", a copy of the survey is sent to the DOE for follow up. The local Title I, Part C Regional Coordinator follows up with all families for whom we receive a Community Survey to determine eligibility for Title I, Part C and provide supplemental services based on their individual needs.
