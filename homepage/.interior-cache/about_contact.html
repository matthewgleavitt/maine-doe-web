Maine DOE Media Consent Form

The Media Consent Form grants the Maine Department of Education permission to use photographs, video, audio recordings, and other media of individuals who complete and sign the form.

To ensure accessibility for as many families as possible, the form is available in the top 30 languages spoken by Maine families.
