What it is

The Maine DOE Commons

The Maine DOE Commons is the department's new blog — a place for the practical, timely content our teams share with Maine schools. It's where you'll find topic articles and how-tos, program and school spotlights, reviews of tools and resources, and curated resource roundups. Unlike the permanent policy pages, this is content that may be more temporal in nature and relevant for updates that don't fit into the newsroom.

Examples from our beta session:

  • Teaching 9/11 — 25th anniversary

How it fits

Commons vs. Newsroom

A quick gut check on where something belongs:

Use the Commons for
Practical, reference material
  • Team-based content for the audience
  • Reviews and spotlights
  • Focus on resources
  • Resource roundups for a topic
  • Interviews / Informational Digests
Use the Newsroom for
News and announcements
  • Press releases and announcements
  • Time-sensitive news
  • Official policy of record
  • Good News
Rule of thumb: if it's news, it goes to the Newsroom. If it's the how-to behind the work, it goes to the Commons. If you have questions about where a piece of content should go, reach out.
Before you post

Determining usage of The Commons

Please discuss with supervisors and directors of your teams and offices to determine if this is the appropriate tool for your content. There has been a gap in ways to share topic-specific content to the field, and this fills that gap — but content should still be thoughtfully produced and mindfully curated. Not posting every day; posting relevant content. The Commons is not a place to editorialize, but rather to share resources with our constituents.

Step by step

How to add to the Commons in Drupal

Draft and edit your text first — a Google Doc or Word is easiest — and have your image ready. Then, in Drupal:

  1. Go to Content → Add content → Blog Post.
  2. Fill in each field (details below), then set "Save as" and click Save.

What goes in each field

FieldRequiredWhat to enter
Post Title Yes The headline. Shows as the large title on the article and on the card in the listing.
Author Yes The byline. Use your team's name (for example, "Maine DOE School Health Team"), not an individual's name.
Body — The article itself. Paste your text in from your draft and use the toolbar to format headings, bold, links, and lists. You can add images inside the body here too.
hero_image — One image shown at the top of the article and on the listing card. PNG, JPG, or GIF, under 5 MB. Must be at least 800×450 pixels; anything larger than 1600×900 is resized automatically. A wide, horizontal (16:9) photo works best.
excerpt Yes A one- to two-sentence teaser. This is what people see on the Commons cards and in the preview when the link is shared. Keep it short and specific.
team Yes Choose your team from the dropdown. If your team isn't listed, let Matt Leavitt know — he'll add it if the team is actively posting.
publish_date Yes The date the post should carry. You can set today's date; it can be changed later if needed.
slug No Leave this blank. The web address is created automatically from the title — you can ignore this field.
Supervisor to Approve — Start typing your supervisor's name and wait for the list to appear, then choose their name. If their name doesn't come up, they don't have a Drupal account yet — email Matt.
Save as — While you're writing, leave this on Draft. When you're ready to hand it off, choose Needs Approval and click Save.
Workflow

How posts get published

Nothing goes live on its own — every post is reviewed first. Here's the flow at a glance, using the statuses you'll see in the "Save as" menu:

StatusWho sets itWhat it means
DraftYou (Author)Still being written. Not visible to anyone but you. Save as Draft as often as you like.
Needs ApprovalYou (Author)You've sent it to your supervisor to review.
Ready for WebSupervisorYour supervisor approved it. It's now queued for Communications to publish.
Send Back for RevisionSupervisorA reviewer wants changes, so it returns to Draft with a note.
PublishedCommunicationsFinal check done — the post is live on the Commons.
ArchivedCommunicationsTaken down from the site later but kept on file.

Who does what

  • You (author): write the post, then set it to Needs Approval to send it to your supervisor.
  • Your supervisor: reviews it, then sets it to Ready for Web to approve, or Send Back for Revision if it needs changes.
  • Communications: does a final check, formats, and Publishes it. This is the only step that puts a post on the live site.

You can open a post any time to see which status it's in.

The emails happen automatically

  • When you send a post for review, the supervisor you chose is emailed right away.
  • When your supervisor approves it, Communications is notified to publish it.
  • When it's published, you get an email letting you know it's live.
For supervisors

Supervisors: you'll need a Drupal account

Reviewing and approving happens in Drupal, so every supervisor who approves for their team needs a Drupal account. If you're a supervisor without one — or a supervisor's name doesn't appear in the Supervisor to Approve field — email Matt Leavitt (matthew.g.leavitt@maine.gov) and he'll set it up.

A few notes

Good to know

  • The main Commons page isn't public yet — we're starting with staff. Individual posts can be shared by direct link for now. Once there's enough content, we'll make a broader communications push. Here's the mock-up of what it will look like once fully available: www.maine.gov/doe/commons
  • Horizontal photos look best as the hero image. Avoid using a screenshot of text as your hero.
  • If you are not currently a Drupal editor but will be using this feature, reach out to Matt to get an account and training on the platform.
  • If you plan to use The Commons, reach out to Matt so he can add your team to the dropdown list (this populates in the URL and will eventually be a filtering category once the full Commons goes live).
  • Save early and often as Draft — you won't lose your work.
  • Questions? Contact Matt Leavitt, Communications (matthew.g.leavitt@maine.gov).
